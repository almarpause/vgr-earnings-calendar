Expected results, revenue releases and trading updates for the companies in the VGR 40, for the current month and the following two. Each entry gives the company and the period reported.

COMPANY Q3-26 Date confirmed by the company COMPANY Q3-26 Estimated date COMPANY Q3-26 Reported

Confirmed means the company, or its exchange filing, has published the specific day. Estimated means the company has not yet published the day; the date is taken from the company's prior-year date or from a third-party calendar and is replaced when the company confirms it.

Labels. Q, H and 9M are the quarter, half-year and nine-month period; FY is the full year. The two digits are the fiscal year as the company names it, so H1-26/27 is the first half of fiscal 2026/27. Times are as stated by the company.

Companies can move a date without notice. The same data is available as CSV, JSON and a calendar feed (ICS). Not investment advice.
